# Security

### Disclosure policy

- Vendano welcomes responsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities. Please report any security concerns privately to support@vendano.net.
- Do not publicly disclose vulnerabilities until coordinated with Vendano’s security team.

### What to include in a disclosure

- A clear description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce, expected vs actual results
- Affected components and environment details
- Your contact information for follow-up

### Response process

- Vendano will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
- A triage team will assess risk and provide a plan for remediation, timeline, and any workarounds.
- Public disclosure will be coordinated with you if and when remediation is complete or a workaround is available.
