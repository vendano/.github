# Vendano

**Retired — active development has ended.**

Vendano was a self-funded experiment in making ADA usable for day-to-day transactions. Over a year and a half, I built and shipped an iPhone app, developed it in public, and explored simpler ways for people to send and accept payments.

The project did not find the sustained adoption or support needed to keep it going, and I have decided to focus on other work.

The application repositories are now private, and the iOS app has been removed from public sale. This repository remains as a public status notice. Vendano is not accepting new contributions or planning further releases.

Existing backend services are staying in place for now; the app itself is no longer actively maintained. Before uninstalling it or clearing its data, securely back up your recovery phrase and verify continued access to any funds independently of the installed app. Never share your recovery phrase or private keys with support.

Thank you to everyone who tried Vendano or offered useful feedback.
