# Vendano: Open, Accessible Cardano Wallet for Everyone

Vendano believes in a world where crypto is approachable, private, and genuinely useful for everyday people. We’re building a Cardano-based mobile wallet that emphasizes instant onboarding, simple transfers, and a transparent, community-driven development process. Our open-source stance isn’t just about code; it’s about inviting everyone to learn, contribute, and co-create a financial tool that fits real human needs.

### Our philosophy

- Open by default: The best ideas come from collaboration. We publish thoughtful designs, share progress openly, and welcome feedback from builders, testers, educators, and new users alike.
- Focus on onboarding: Cardano can be powerful, but new users shouldn’t need to decipher long addresses or jargon. Vendano puts first-time users at the center with clear guidance and frictionless setup.
- Privacy as a feature: Personal data should stay private. We design flows that minimize data exposure, employ hashed identifiers for contact-based sending, and keep sensitive information off-chain where possible.
- Educational by design: We’re not just shipping a wallet; we’re teaching a mental model for how crypto works and how self-custody feels in practice.
- Community-led growth: Adoption isn’t a solo sprint. We grow by sharing knowledge, inviting contributions, and building a community that reflects diverse needs and perspectives.

### The problem we’re solving

Cardano newcomers often confront long addresses, complex terminology, and a learning curve that can discourage exploration. Vendano aims to make it easy to:

- Set up a wallet instantly
- Get started with minimal jargon and clear steps
- Send ADA to a friend using a phone number or email (via hashed contact matching)
- Learn by doing with a safe, low-friction entry point

### The Vendano approach

- Simple onboarding: Seed words are shown at first launch to establish self-custody clarity; funding paths are explained in plain language.
- Everyday transfers: A send flow that uses phone numbers or emails to locate recipient addresses through privacy-preserving hashes.
- Self-custody as default: Users control their keys from day one, with guidance that reduces risk and confusion.
- Transparent metrics: We instrument onboarding funnels and publish progress to the community, so results are observable and verifiable.

### Who this is for

- New crypto users who want a gentle, approachable entry point
- Educators and mentors teaching crypto fundamentals
- Developers and researchers interested in privacy-preserving, user-friendly flows
- Community members who want to review, critique, and improve open-source wallet design

### How to contribute

We welcome contributions in many forms: code, UX critique, documentation, security reviews, and community testing. See CONTRIBUTING.md for how to get involved, how to run checks, and how to propose changes.

### Governance, transparency, and updates

- We publish regular community updates on milestones, learnings, and planned next steps.
- All core designs and decisions are documented and discussed with the community before significant changes.
- Open channels for feedback ensure the project evolves with real user needs.

### Quick-start for new contributors

1. Read CONTRIBUTING.md to understand how to propose changes.
2. Explore the codebases and issue tracker to find a starting point.
3. Join discussions, provide UX feedback, or submit a small, self-contained enhancement.
4. Follow the project’s security and privacy guidelines when reviewing or proposing changes.
