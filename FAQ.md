# Frequently asked questions

## What is Vendano's status?

Vendano was an experiment in everyday ADA payments. Active development has ended. The iOS app has been removed from public sale, and the application repositories are private. See the [organization profile](profile/README.md) and [website](https://vendano.net/) for the full status.

## I previously used Vendano. What should I do?

Before uninstalling Vendano or clearing its data, securely back up your recovery phrase and verify that you can still access any funds without relying on the installed app. Never send your recovery phrase or private keys to anyone, including support.
