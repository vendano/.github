# Code of Conduct

Vendano is committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all participants in Vendano’s open-source efforts.

### Guiding principles

- Be kind and courteous to others.
- Be mindful of different backgrounds, perspectives, and skill levels.
- Respect privacy and data protection when discussing vulnerabilities or incidents.
- Refrain from harassing, abusive, or discriminatory behavior.

### Reporting and escalation

- If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact jeffrey@pushplay.net.
- For security concerns, see SECURITY.md for vulnerability reporting guidance.
- Violations may lead to temporary or permanent removal from contributing, per the maintainers’ discretion.

### Scope

- This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions within Vendano’s public templates repository and related communications.
